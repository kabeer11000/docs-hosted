The Grass

She started texting me while sitting right in front of me.

Then she came over and sat beside me. We shared playlists. It was kind of cute.

The Concert In Our Rooms

Travis Scott's beats filled the air.

We sang. Not just the words — the spaces between them.

We fell asleep connected.

Somewhere across the city. Still together.

November 7 — The Bouquet

The satisfaction of seeing her smile when I handed her the bouquet was enough of a reward.

November 22 — The End

She had a long list of questions.

I answered every question except one.

"What do you want us to be now?"

I teared up. I didn't have an answer.

"I don't think I can be with you. It hurts too much."

I love her, with every fiber of my being.

And I know she loves me too.

I just messed it all up.
